Delete Your Tervive Account

Tervive is developed by SoftServeGaming.

Option 1: Delete your account from inside the app

If you still have Tervive installed, this is the fastest way:

  1. Open Tervive and log in.
  2. Tap your profile icon (top of the main screen) to open the profile menu.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

This immediately and permanently deletes your login credentials and signs you out. It cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you've uninstalled Tervive or no longer have access to it, email us and we'll delete your account for you:

Email: softnationgaming@gmail.com

Please send this request from the email address associated with your Tervive account, and include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line. We'll confirm once your account and data have been deleted.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Login credentials (email, password)Permanently deleted
Player profile (name, progress, stats)Permanently deleted
Saved runs / game savesPermanently deleted

Deletion happens immediately and is not recoverable. We do not retain a backup copy after deletion.

Partial deletion (keep your account, reset your progress)

If you only want to reset your game progress without deleting your account entirely, open the profile menu in-app and choose Delete Profile instead of Delete Account. This erases your saved progress and returns you to the first-login screen, but keeps your login credentials intact so you can start fresh.
